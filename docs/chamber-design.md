## World rhythm

- Velvet Drain teaches current previews, valve timing, nonlethal drain pressure,
  and a short chain-clear window.
- Heartbreak Hotel makes route choice explicit through previewed mirrors,
  moving lifts and revealed platforms. Mirror exits grant a brief safety window.
- Jade Garden rewards preserving a bubble. Moon pools alter buoyancy, controlled
  chains grow vines, and roses or buds open at stated chain thresholds.
- Crimson Chapel turns readable orders and enemy combinations into geometry.
  Thorn warnings always precede active frames; the safe route never requires a
  ritual clear.
- The Black Bubble recombines learned systems. Gravity zones have text and arrow
  labels, phase destinations are ghosted, and time replays warn before impact.

## Encore set

Five post-story chambers unlock after the first dawn clear. They are optional,
lore-free score routes and award `clear`, `noDamage`, `targetScore`, and
`fullChain` medals. No Encore medal gates the ending, canonical fragments, the
Dirty Gold Vault, hero mastery, or core unlocks.
